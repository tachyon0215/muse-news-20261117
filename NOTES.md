# News research — Tuesday 17 November 2026

Five stories chosen for consequence, spread across regions/fields, and simple visual expressibility.
All events happened on, or were first reported on, 17 November 2026.

## 1. War / Europe — Bridges hit in the Russia–Ukraine war
- **Headline:** BRIDGES HIT IN UKRAINE WAR
- **Sublabel:** Ukraine damages Crimea bridge; Kyiv bridges struck
- **What happened:** Russian ballistic missiles struck Kyiv's North and South bridges over the Dnipro late Monday, disrupting heating, water and power, while a Ukrainian strike collapsed a section of the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy hailed "an unprecedented operation" weeks in the making.
- **Source:** Zark News Daily Briefing: November 17, 2026 — https://zarknews.com/zark-news-daily-briefing-november-17-2026/
- **Visual:** A bridge over water with a broken, tilted middle span and an explosion burst.

## 2. Diplomacy / Asia-Pacific — Quad leaders' summit in Manila
- **Headline:** QUAD LEADERS MEET IN MANILA
- **Sublabel:** First summit since 2024; mineral deals agreed
- **What happened:** US President Trump, Japan's PM Takaichi, India's PM Modi and Australia's PM Albanese met in Manila for the first in-person Quad leaders' summit since 2024 (and the first hosted in a non-Quad country), announcing initiatives on critical minerals, maritime surveillance, cables and cyber cooperation.
- **Source:** The White House, Quad Leaders' Summit Joint Press Release (Nov 17, 2026) — https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/quad-leaders-summit-joint-press-release/
- **Visual:** Four circles labelled US / JP / IN / AU arranged as one grouping.

## 3. Security / Middle East — Iran drones target a US radar site in Bahrain
- **Headline:** IRAN DRONES TARGET BAHRAIN BASE
- **Sublabel:** Bahrain says drones intercepted; no casualties reported
- **What happened:** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps said it fired drones at a US military radar system at the Sheikh Isa base in Bahrain; Bahrain said it intercepted the attack, with no reports of casualties or damage. A third US carrier strike group had also just arrived in the region.
- **Source:** Just Security, Early Edition: November 17, 2026 — https://www.justsecurity.org/164914/early-edition-november-17-2026/
- **Visual:** A quadcopter drone above a radar dish with red signal arcs.

## 4. Economy / Technology — Nvidia sales double
- **Headline:** NVIDIA SALES DOUBLE AGAIN
- **Sublabel:** Revenue hits $115.8 billion on AI demand
- **What happened:** Nvidia reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $115.8 billion after the bell on Nov 17 — up 103% year on year, with data-centre revenue of $107.6 billion — beating estimates and guiding Q4 to $130 billion. It was the chipmaker's first report since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO.
- **Source:** The Motley Fool, "Nvidia Sales Double Under an Interim CEO" (Nov 17, 2026) — https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/17/nvidia-sales-double-under-an-interim-ceo-rapid-earnings-reaction/
- **Visual:** A green AI computer chip with pins and a rising arrow.

## 5. Nature / Environment — Christmas Island red crab migration
- **Headline:** RED CRABS MARCH TO SEA
- **Sublabel:** Tens of millions cross Christmas Island roads
- **What happened:** Tens of millions of red crabs began their annual migration across Australia's Christmas Island — roads were closed and crab bridges used — marching from the forest to the Indian Ocean to breed, timed to the wet-season rains and the lunar cycle. Footage from Parks Australia was published/reported on Nov 17 (migration underway from Nov 14–15).
- **Source:** Fox Weather, "Millions of red crabs take over Australia's Christmas Island" (published Nov 17, 2026) — https://www.foxweather.com/earth-space/video-millions-crabs-australia-christmas-island-begin-mass-migration.amp
- **Visual:** Several bold red crabs walking across a road toward blue sea.
